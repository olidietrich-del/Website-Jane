ABOUT ME

Jane is an Irish professional freelance violinist based between Vienna and Dublin. She performs regularly with the National Symphony Orchestra, Irish Chamber Orchestra, RTE Concert Orchestra, Wexford Festival Opera and Irish National Opera, and has also performed with ORF Radio Symphony Orchestra as part of WienModerne Festival in Vienna.
