ABOUT ME

I am a violinist with a background in classical music performance.

Since completion of BA and MA Performance Degrees in the Royal Irish Academy of Music in Dublin and the University of Limerick respectively, my work has led me in varying directions from regular performances with Ireland’s leading orchestras to devising and premiering new contemporary works, broadcasting children’s radio, co-creating performative cross collaborative shows internationally and exploring many ways in which the violin and body can produce sound. Over the past several years, my practice aims to bring together classical and experimental musical engagement with community groups in order to create accessible structures within the wider music ecosystem. This work has led to multisensory performances with the Deaf community, children and those with chronic conditions.
